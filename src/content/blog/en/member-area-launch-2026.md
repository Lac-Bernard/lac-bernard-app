The **online member area** on [lacbernard.ca](https://lacbernard.ca) is ready. Members can check their membership status, keep contact details up to date, and this year **pay securely by credit card** from the website—something many of you have asked for. Thank you for your patience while we put this in place.

## What you need to sign in

Use the **same email address the association already has on file as your main (primary) contact email**. That way, when you sign in, the site can connect you to your existing member record, including your **2026** membership status and **past years** on file, if we have them.

If you use a different email than the one in our records, the site may not find your profile. Try another address with “Use a different email” on the sign-in page, or contact us and we’ll help align your email.

## How to sign in

- **English:** [Sign in (English)](https://lacbernard.ca/en/membership/account/sign-in)  
- **French:** [Connexion (français)](https://lacbernard.ca/fr/membership/account/sign-in)

You can **Continue with Google** or enter your email and choose **Send magic link**. We’ll email you a one-time link (**no password to remember**). If you use Google, that Google email should be the same one we have as your primary contact.

## What you’ll see

After you sign in, you’ll land in your **member area**. You should see a short summary of your profile and your **membership status for the current calendar year** (the association follows the usual **January–December** year, based on **Eastern time**). When applicable, you’ll also see **earlier years** and anything **prepaid** for future years.

## What you can do there

- **Update your profile:** lake address, phones, mailing address, and optional secondary contact.  
- **Start or renew** your membership for the year when you’re not yet active.  
- **Pay** from the same screen where you pick your type: **Pay with credit card** (optional donation in the window that opens, then Stripe checkout) or **Pay another way** (Interac, cheque, or cash—instructions appear after the page updates; you can add an optional donation with the same offline payment—see the note on screen).

## Paying by credit card

**Pay with credit card** takes you through a short, secure checkout (handled by Stripe). You can add an **optional donation** to support the association in the same step if you wish. When payment succeeds, your membership should show as **active** shortly after.

If you prefer not to use a card, choose **Pay another way** on your account; the page will show everything you need for **e-Transfer, cheque, or cash**, including how to note an **optional donation** (and you can still switch to card later from the pending section if you want).

## Quick steps: buy your 2026 membership online

1. Open the **sign-in** link above (pick English or French).  
2. Sign in with **Google** or your **magic link** email.  
3. If the site asks you to **create or complete your profile**, fill it in and save, especially your **lake address** if you want **Voting** membership.  
4. On your **account** page, choose **Voting** or **Associate** (non-voting), then click **Pay with credit card** (complete checkout) **or** **Pay another way** (then follow the on-screen instructions).  

If you already have an active 2026 membership, you’ll see that instead of the payment flow.
